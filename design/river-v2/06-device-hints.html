THE FM-1'S BUTTONS, IN A CARD

Today the buttons that lead to each page (EDIT, ENV, LFO, ARP…) are drawn exactly like the River's own buttons: a frame, caps, a radius. They look clickable, and they are not. In v2 they become a diagram: the FM-1's block of twelve buttons, tiny, with the ones that lead there lit, and their names as quiet text. Nothing framed, nothing that reacts to the mouse: information about the instrument, not a control of the page.

HOW TO READ IT

The twelve buttons at the FM-1's right, as they sit on its panel. DRONE's pages are under EDIT, ENV and LFO, so those three are lit, and their names follow in quiet text. In an opened section each page also names its own button, under its name. The map is never framed, never reacts to the mouse: it reads as a picture of the instrument.
