EDITOR

An opened card is also an editor. Every setting turns as on the FM-1: drag it left or right (or the arrow keys), and a choice opens its list. The sound follows at once, and nothing changes in the shard until you save it as a new one. Each change goes through FRASIL's own engine, as a MIDI CC learned on that setting would, so a preset's sound or CUSTOM after a drone's setting is turned behave exactly as on the device. These pictures were made that way: ABYSS here really plays its edited sound, and its LFO 1, woken up, moves the choir.
