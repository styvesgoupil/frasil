# FRASIL in the host simulator: MASTER EQ turned while a scene plays, no click (3.1, Styves on 3.1.0-b5: "when I
# change the HIGH values, it crackles"): ./build.sh --host, then
#   app/build/host/frasil_host app/tests/eq_turn.txt app/build/host
# The bands' coefficients glided a block at a time (256 frames), each block's step a click at its edge: a shelf's
# large b0 against the states the coefficients before made, HIGH the most, MID next. Now a straight line over 20 ms,
# a sample at a time (frasil.c eq_chunk, eq_glide). Measured (edges, expect edgedb: the output's second difference
# squared at the blocks' first samples over their middles', dB x 10), factory scene 3 playing, its sequences and a
# chord; "still": the same script, each turn a wait as long (the scene's own edges, which a raised treble lifts):
#                     at rest   HIGH quick   HIGH slow   LOW   MID   the presets   HIGH FREQ
#   still                 7          8           34       49    49        1           -1
#   before (3.1.0-b6)     7        401          206       48   111      302          266
#   now                   7         14           23       47    38        1            0
# (a band at rest renders as before, bit for bit)
keysstay 1
master 4096
wait 300
midi C0 03
wait 500
tap PLAY
midi 90 48 80
midi 90 55 80
midi 90 64 80
wait 3000
gopage 40
wait 100
expect page 40
# the bands flat, the EQ CUSTOM: at rest
turn K1 1
wait 300
expect p130 1
edges
wait 2000
expect edgedb <30
# -- HIGH, quick (a detent every 12 ms, the turn's speed doubling them): 0 to +12, to -12, up again; then 0 (unmeasured)
edges
turn K4 24 12
expect p133 48
turn K4 -48 12
expect p133 0
turn K4 24 12
wait 300
expect edgedb <30
param 133 24
wait 300
# -- HIGH, as a hand turns it (a detent every 80 ms)
edges

expect p133 0
turn K4 24
wait 300

edges

turn K2 24 12
wait 300

edges
