# The SMK-37's hands moved by a button or a wheel (card #111, Styves): KNOB BANK the track, the mod wheel the track's
# ALGORITHM setting (the drone's TIMBRE, GLINT's BRIGHT, the bass's FILTER; on the LEAD its vibrato, nothing else). The
# play page names them in a key over TRACK's label and the setting's (the LEAD's FILTER: K7's badge); the large view
# names the control that moved it, KNOB BANK or MOD WHEEL (handby 1, 2), its knob's badge after a knob (0). Shots:
# OUTDIR/smk-home-*.png. On the SMK-37's simulator alone (its KBANK, its wheels):
#   FM1B_BOARD=smk37 FM1B_MODEL=elite ./build.sh --host
#   app/build/host/frasil-smk37-elite_host app/tests/smk37_hands.txt OUTDIR
# settings: the bass's FILTER 56 (0 .. 127), P_KB 166 (0 DRONE, 1 GLINT, 2 BASS, 3 LEAD; MIDI: LEAD's, mout 1)
setp 214 1
setp 216 0
setp 217 0
setp 224 0
wait 1600
gopage 0
wait 1500
expect p166 0
expect handby 0
shot smk-home.png
# KNOB BANK: GLINT, the large view KNOB BANK's
tap KBANK
wait 100
expect p166 1
expect overlay 1
expect handby 1
shot smk-home-kbank.png
# K6 (SELECT) turned: BASS, its badge again
wait 1500
turn SELECT 1
wait 100
expect p166 2
expect handby 0
wait 1500
# the mod wheel on BASS: its FILTER where the wheel is (127 of 127: the top), the large view MOD WHEEL's
wheel mod 127
wait 100
expect p56 127
expect overlay 1
expect handby 2
shot smk-home-modwheel.png
# K7 (ALGORITHM) turned: its badge again
wait 1500
turn ALGO -1
wait 100
expect p56 126
expect handby 0
# KNOB BANK again: the LEAD; the mod wheel moved: its vibrato, not its FILTER (the large view nothing new)
wait 1500
tap KBANK
wait 100
expect p166 3
expect handby 1
wait 1500
wheel mod 0
wait 100
expect handby 1
wait 1500
shot smk-home-lead.png
# KNOB BANK: MIDI (card #164), its hands TRACK MIDI, K5 CHANNEL, K7 OCTAVE (KNOB BANK's key over TRACK)
tap KBANK
wait 100
expect p166 3
expect mout 1
expect handby 1
shot smk-home-midi-kbank.png
wait 1500
shot smk-home-midi.png
# KNOB BANK round: DRONE again
tap KBANK
wait 100
expect p166 0
expect mout 0
expect handby 1
