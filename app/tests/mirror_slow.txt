# The screen mirror at USB's own pace (card #279; mirror_rate 48: a 64-byte USB-MIDI packet a millisecond), the river
# moving: the link full, frames dropped, redraws asked. What a viewer reads arrives all the same: the header, the
# footer, an edit page's values under the river within half a second of a turn (mirror.sh compares those rows: NAME
# ROWS, below), the logo's every row, a pop-up and the help whole; ANIMATIONS LOW, the whole screen. A viewer comes on
# each (mirror out:).
#   app/tests/mirror.sh OUTDIR
master 3000
# the logo's screen at power-on, alive: every row reaches the viewer (its water moving all over: logo:covers)
mirror out:logo.syx
wait 3600
fbdump logo.raw
mirror off
wait 1400
tapkey 9 90
wait 300
tapkey 2 80
wait 1000
# the river: the header and the footer (rows 0-31, 176-239)
mirror out:river.syx
wait 2000
fbdump river.raw
mirror off
# an edit page (DRONE I: CHOIR .. WARMTH under the river): WARMTH turned, its row and the others, 500 ms on (rows
# 0-31, 96-239); turned back, the same
turn K1 1
wait 1000
mirror out:edit.syx
wait 2000
turn K4 9
wait 500
fbdump edit.raw
mirror out:edit_back.syx
wait 2000
turn K4 -9
wait 500
fbdump edit_back.raw
mirror off
# a pop-up over everything (STOP FIRST), whole
backup out:playing.frasil
mirror out:popup.syx
wait 1500
restore out:playing.frasil
wait 1500
expect popup 1
fbdump popup.raw
mirror out:popup_gone.syx
tap SEL
wait 1500
expect popup 0
fbdump popup_gone.raw
mirror off
# the help, whole
mirror out:help.syx
