# FRASIL in the host simulator: the shedding (app.c load_watch), on a load the script sets: ./build.sh --host, then
#   app/build/host/frasil_host app/tests/shed.txt app/build/host
# cpuload N: the load the simulator reads, N % (never the Mac's time: a loaded Mac shed the sound and the tests' values
# changed from pass to pass, card #85; the FM-1 and the web page measure theirs). Over 82 %: a step lighter, one a
# second, up to 8 (FR_SHED_TOP: the LEAD's three steps more, card #144, admin #741). At 75 % or less: a step given back
# after a calm, 4 s at first, twice as long when the load came back within 15 s of a step given back.
keysstay 1
wait 600
expect shed 0
cpuload 90
wait 600
expect shed 1
wait 1300
expect shed 2
wait 3000
expect shed 5
wait 2000
expect shed 7
wait 2000
expect shed 8
wait 2000
expect shed 8
# the load down: the steps given back, one every 4 s (the last step's 4 s first)
cpuload 60
wait 3000
expect shed 8
wait 2000
expect shed 7
wait 4000
expect shed 6
# up again soon after a step given back: a step lighter at once, the next calm 8 s
cpuload 90
wait 200
expect shed 7
cpuload 0
wait 10000
expect shed 7
wait 3000
expect shed 6
wait 6000
expect shed 6
wait 2000
expect shed 5
