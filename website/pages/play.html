The same code the FM-1 runs, with sound.

No FM-1 needed. Switch it on, then tap a key: a drone rises and stays. Tap it again and it fades. Use headphones if you can: the drones are long, and the bass is deep.

How to play it here

First sounds

  1. Press Switch on.
  2. Tap a key on the lower row: a drone rises. Tap two or three more.
  3. Turn PRESETS: 24 drones to choose from. ALGORITHM thins the drone out, or fills it.
  4. Turn SELECT to GLINT: the keys play small icy bells over the drones. REC records them into GLINT’s sequencer, live.
  5. Hold PLAY to release everything. SAVE opens the scenes.

Good to know

Tab moves through the knobs, the buttons and the keys; the arrow keys turn a knob. Pressure, on the FM-1 the heart of it, is the one thing a mouse can’t give: here a key is pressed or not.

Shift-click or right-click a key or a button to keep it held until you click it again: for SEL and a key, SAVE and REC, or to hold ARP.

Over the panel, FRASIL buttons writes what each control does here (SEQ: BASS, ARP: GLINT, SAVE: SCENES); FM-1 buttons, the names printed on the FM-1.

What you save stays in this browser. Factory reset forgets it.
