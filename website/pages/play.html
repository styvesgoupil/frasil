The same code the FM-1 runs, with sound.

No FM-1 needed. Switch it on, tap a key: a drone rises and stays. Best in headphones.

How to play it here

First sounds

  1. Press Switch on.
  2. Tap two or three keys on the lower row.
  3. Turn PRESETS: 24 drones.
  4. Turn SELECT to GLINT: the keys play icy bells.
  5. Hold PLAY to release everything.
