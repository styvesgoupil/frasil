Riverlibrary

Your scenes, as shards of ice.

FRASIL River is a library for your FM-1’s scenes. In the River a scene is a shard: its drone, what it plays and every page’s settings, on a card you can play in the browser with FRASIL’s own engine.

Go to FRASIL River →

  1. Your Private River

    Manage your shards

    Grab the scenes from your FM-1, name them, save them and your backups online, and put any of them back on your FM-1, into the slots you choose, from any computer.

  2. Community River

    Share them, browse the others’

    Share a shard and it drifts there for every player, under CC BY 4.0. Play the shards others shared, send one to your FM-1, save the ones you love.

  3. Tools

    Back up to a file, restore

    No account needed: back up the sixteen scenes and the settings to a file, open it to see each shard, and restore it whole or shard by shard.

Chrome or Edge on a computer, the FM-1 on USB, FRASIL 1.1 or later. An account is free; it only needs an email.
