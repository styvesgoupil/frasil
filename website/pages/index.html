The instrument1/11

Infinite, evolving,
richly textured soundscapes.

FRASIL turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into an instrument for ambient drones. Tap a key: a drone latches and drifts on its own, into a large reverb with shimmer and freeze.

Icy bells above it, a deep bass under it. The notes stay; the sound never stops moving.
