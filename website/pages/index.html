The instrument1/11

The notes stay where you put them. The sound is never still.

FRASIL turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into an instrument for ambient drones and textures. A tap latches a drone: a note, or a chord built in the key you chose. Pressing harder makes it swell. The timbre drifts and ebbs on its own, slowly, and everything flows into a large, lush reverb with shimmer and freeze.

Your notes, and chance where you let it

The drones are the notes you light, and they never change. Above them, GLINT plays small icy bells; under them, BASS holds one deep note, or walks a slow line of its own. GLINT’s arpeggiator plays the keys you hold; GLINT’s sequencer and the bass’s play the steps you write. Chance comes in only where you let it: CHANCE skips steps, RANDOM and DRIFT pick the arpeggio’s order, SPARKLE lets glints fall on the notes your drones hold.

Who it is for

For players who want a sound to hold rather than a pattern to follow. FRASIL is designed as one voice in a larger setup: it takes MIDI notes, pressure, CCs and clock over USB and TRS, a channel for each of its tracks.

Layers3/11

EDIT

×7: the drone, each layer and its second page

The current, the crystals, the mist.

Every note you light is a voice, up to eight, and every voice has three layers. All three play every note: you mix them and shape each one. On the screen each is a thing in the river, and strata under the waterline show the exact mix, WARMTH with it.

  1. CHOIR the current

    Three detuned saws, or pulses, through a low-pass filter that follows the note. Warm, wide, vocal.

    CHOIR II SHAPE takes the saws toward a square, hollow and woody, then narrow pulses, bright and nasal. INTERVAL moves the centre saw an octave or a fifth; RESONANCE and TYPE shape the filter: LOW, BAND or HIGH.

  2. PARTIALS the crystals

    Eight additive harmonics, each drifting on its own in level and tuning. Bright or dark, odd, stretched into bells, with a formant.

    PARTIALS II EVOLVE lets their levels wander deeper and quicker; BEAT makes each one pulse at its own pace. FORMANT and PEAK lift them around one harmonic, as a vowel does, up to that one alone: the drone sings a single overtone.

  3. BREATH the mist

    Noise, a hiss or sparse grains, through three resonators tuned to the note, from air to glass.

    BREATH II GRAIN turns the hiss into grains that ring like ice ticking, or rain on glass. PITCH moves the resonators an octave down, or up into a halo over the drone.

Cloud4/11

FX

Again: cloud · Hold: freeze

Everything flows into the cloud.

WARMTH, a soft saturation, feeds CLOUD, the reverb: eight modulated lines with diffusion inside their loop, a pre-delay, and a shimmer pitched inside the loop. Its tail runs from 0.4 to 60 seconds.

Generative FX

FX opens on four knobs that play the whole sound. SWELL swells every voice and holds it there; SPARKLE lets glints fall by themselves, now and then, on the notes the drones hold; BLUR dissolves the drone into the cloud; SINK takes everything under, a low-pass on the whole mix.

Freeze

Hold FX: the cloud holds what is in it, without end and without darkening. Nothing more goes in: what you play next sounds dry over it. Hold FX again and it thaws, fading over DECAY. On the screen, the crystals set into a lattice and the water stills.

Glint5/11

ARP

Hold: arp latch · REC: record

Small icy bells, above the drones.

GLINT is four voices of clean two-operator FM, played from the keys once SELECT turns them over to it. Never latched: each note rings and fades like a bell, while the drones keep sounding under it.

2SEQ

1MODE

Tap the sky: a glint, and its ping-pong echoes

The hand

Pressure makes a glint louder and longer. With VIBRATO, a wiggle of the finger bends its pitch, both ways around the note, as a string player’s does; a steady press stays in tune. SUSTAIN turns a bell into a lead.

The arpeggiator

It plays the GLINT keys you hold, one at a time, at RATE, over RANGE octaves, on the grid of MIDI clock or TEMPO. LATCH is on from the start: the pattern keeps playing after you lift your hands. Hold ARP to turn it off, or on again. Tap PLAY and it waits; again, it goes on from the top.

The sequencer

SEQ on SEQ (GLINT I, K2): 32 steps in place of the arpeggio, on the same grid. REC goes straight there and records you live as it runs, to a metronome; or write it a key a step. With RECORD off, the keys transpose it along the scale. PLAY stops it; again, it starts from the top. A NEW TAKE starts it over from nothing, and UNDO brings back the one before.

The delay

GLINT’s own, tempo-synced, from 1/32 to 1/2 with triplets and dotted times, mono or ping-pong. Its echoes go into the cloud too.

Bass6/11

SEQ

Select: bass · REC: record

One deep note, held like a drone.

Turn SELECT to BASS and the keys play it: one note at a time, the key’s note in the scale, OCTAVE octaves down. Tap another key and it glides there without starting again; tap its own key and it lets go. Press into it: louder, and its filter opens.

Or let it walk. The bass has a step sequencer of its own, two more pages under SEQ: sixteen steps, each a NOTE, a CUT or a HOLD, and each as long as you want it, from a sixteenth to four bars. A slow line under drones that never change. Write it step by step or live with REC, move it with a key; PLAY starts it with GLINT’s, on the same grid, and each scene keeps its own.

DRIVE into a soft clip, a resonant low-pass, and none of it in the reverb. A pure sine this low barely reaches the FM-1’s small speaker: DRIVE, or a richer MODEL, gives it the harmonics that let you hear it there. In headphones, SINE with DRIVE at 0 is a clean sub.

Scenes · MIDI9/11

SAVE

Play: load · Save + REC: store

Sixteen scenes. A channel for each track.

A scene holds the sound, the latched drones, GLINT’s arpeggio and its sequence, and the bass with its own. Hold SAVE and press REC to store one; hold SAVE and press a white key to load one of the sixteen, from any page. MORPH glides into it, over up to two minutes, and LOAD on SND ONLY leaves what plays playing while only the sound changes. Eight slots hold the factory scenes; eight are free.

MIDI over USB and TRS

Each track has its own channel, whatever the keys play. The drones’ notes go through the harmony, so a sequencer’s single note plays a chord; GLINT’s and BASS’s play as they come. Polyphonic aftertouch swells a note; channel pressure, the whole track. Program Change loads the scenes. Hold GLO and move a knob on your controller: it now turns the setting you touched last. Clock drives GLINT’s arpeggiator, its sequencer and its delay, the bass’s sequencer, and the synced LFOs; Start resets the grid and starts the sequences.
