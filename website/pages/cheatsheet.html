The panel

DRONE HOME · SELECT A left

TAP
lights a note or chord; it stays.
TAP AGAIN
it fades over RELEASE.
PRESS IN
it swells and opens.
SEL + KEY
a single note over a chord.
ENV HELD
SWELL: every voice at once.
PLAY HELD
RELEASE ALL; again: RESTORE. Held, or starting: the beat restarts.
KEY MODE
GLOBAL, K4: LATCH or HOLD.

GLINT ARP · SELECT A middle

KEY
an icy FM bell; it rings out.
PRESS IN
louder; a wiggle: VIBRATO.
SEQ
OFF, ARP, or SEQ (32 steps): GLINT I, K2.
ARP HELD
LATCH (on: SEQ on ARP): plays on.
REC
records LIVE into the sequence; again: off; held: metronome.
SEQ, KEY
transposed, from step 1 on the beat; on its SEQ pages, a glint.

BASS SEQ · SELECT A right

TAP
holds one note, OCTAVE down.
OTHER KEY
it glides there (GLIDE).
ITS KEY
it lets go.
PRESS IN
louder; its filter opens.
IT STAYS
till PLAY is held.
PULSE
struck again on the grid.
MODEL
SINE, TRIANGLE, SQUARE, SAW, REESE, SUB, ORGAN, FM.
SEQUENCE
16 steps; REC: as you play.

SCENES16 slots · 1–7 factory · NEW SHARD: start fresh SAVE

SAVE
SCENES: K1 or A the slot, K2 MORPH, K3 LOAD.
SAVE + REC
STORE, in K1’s slot on SCENES, else in the current scene.
NAME IT
then, 16 characters: OCT− OCT+ the cursor, K4 letters, K3 digits; SEL stores it, FX cancels.
SAVE + KEY
loads: the 16 white keys are the 16 slots. On SCENES: PLAY.
MORPH
CUT (at power-on), or 1 S to 2:00: the sound glides into the scene.
LOAD
SND+NOTES: all let go, its notes play. SND ONLY: the sound alone, what plays goes on.
EDIT HELD
COMPARE: the scene as saved. SAVE lit: it has CHANGED.
