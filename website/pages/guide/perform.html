Open it

Plug the FM-1 in by USB, open perform.frasilsynth.com in Chrome or Edge, and press F for full screen.

The river fills the screen, drawn from what the FM-1 plays. Under it:

  • LIVE: what your hand does: a page, a knob and its value, a scene, the keys.
  • The LFOs: each one’s shape and what it moves.
  • CLOUD, TAPE, FILTER: the effects drawn, lit while you turn them.

The first screen

  • ENTER YOUR ARTIST NAME: shown under the scene, after BY.
  • FM-1 AUDIO or COMPUTER AUDIO: where you hear it. COMPUTER AUDIO plays the FM-1 through the computer (allow the audio input) and mutes the FM-1 while connected.
  • CONNECT finds the FM-1 (allow MIDI). DEMO, without an FM-1: the factory scenes play in the browser, an AUTO hand turning the knobs.

The header

The logo, the scene’s name, and the transport: DR, GL, BS. A lit triangle while a track plays, a dim square when stopped. Under each: ARP, SEQ, or the KEY MODE.

What it costs the FM-1

Nothing you can hear. The FM-1 only talks while a page is open.

PERFORM needs FRASIL 2.5 or later. If the page doesn’t find the FM-1, see Troubleshooting.
