The gestures

GestureWhat happens
Tap a keyLights the note, or a chord (see Harmony). It rises over ATTACK and stays
Tap a lit keyIt fades over RELEASE
Hold a key and pressThe voice swells and opens (PRESSURE sets how much). Let go: it stays lit
Hold SEQSWELL: after 0.4 s, every voice swells together (1.5 s up, 1.5 s down)
SWELL (FX, K1)Held where you leave it: every voice swells as far as it is turned, and stays there. With SEQ held or CC 11 too, the most of them wins. Scenes don’t keep it
Turn SELECTKEYBOARD, what the keys play: DRONE, GLINT or BASS (the badge at the top right says which, in its colour). Scenes don’t keep it
Turn PRESETSThe drone’s preset: CUSTOM, then the 24 (see the drone’s presets)
Turn ALGORITHMFULLNESS: at 100 % the drone as its settings make it; down, thinner (see the whole drone)
Hold SEL, tap a keyA single note, even when CHORD makes chords: a note over a chord
OCT− · OCT+Octave −3 to +2. Both together: back to −1, where the keys run F2 to G4
Hold PLAYAfter 0.6 s, RELEASE ALL: the drones, the bass, the glints, the arpeggio and the sequence. From 0.2 s the header says RELEASE ALL, a line counting down under it: let go then, before 0.6 s, and nothing happens
Hold PLAY againWith nothing playing: RESTORE, what the last RELEASE ALL let go comes back (the latched drones, the bass, the arpeggio’s notes, the sequence running)
Tap PLAYGLINT’s transport: the sequence stops, or starts from its first step; the arpeggio waits, or goes on from the top. With TYPE OFF it only says HOLD PLAY · RELEASE ALL
RECInto GLINT’s sequencer, recording LIVE (see the sequencer). Again: RECORD off, the keys back to what they played before REC. It flashes on the beat while recording, with a metronome; hold it to turn the metronome off or on
Hold FX (or CLOUD’s K4)FREEZE: the cloud holds what is in it, without end and without darkening. Nothing more goes in: what you play next sounds dry over it. Off: it thaws, and fades over DECAY. The page stays; FX stays bright while it is on

With GLOBAL › KEYS on HOLD, notes sound only while their key is held.

The buttons

A press shows, a hold acts. Every button does its job when you let it go, but OCT− and OCT+, which act at once. Held 0.4 s (PLAY 0.6 s), it does its other thing instead. A button used with something else (SEL with a key, SAVE with a key or REC, LFO with an encoder, GLO with a CC) does nothing more when you let it go.

The shape of a drone

ENV opens DRONE SHAPE: ATTACK (5 ms to 30 s), RELEASE (50 ms to 60 s), PRESSURE (its effect) and VELOCITY. LFO opens DRONE MOTION: MOTION (the drift’s depth), RATE (20 s to 0.5 s), TIDE (a swell of the timbre) and PERIOD (5 min to 10 s). Press LFO again for LFO 1, LFO 2 and LFO 3, three LFOs you set on the sound yourself: see The LFOs.

The three encoders on the left do the same on every page: A KEYBOARD, B the drone’s PRESET, C its FULLNESS. The drone’s TIMBRE, SPACE and MOTION are on the first page of EDIT, DRONE PARAMS, with FULLNESS again. TEMPO is on GLOBAL, K1.
