The buttons

A button acts when you let it go (OCT− and OCT+ at once). Hold it for its other action. Used in a combo, it does nothing more on release.

PLAY restarts the beat when it starts, releases, restores or cuts: the LFOs, PUMP, the arpeggio and the sequences start from the top. With a MIDI clock in, the clock keeps the beat.

KEYS follow the section. ARP’s pages turn the keys to GLINT, SEQ’s to BASS, EDIT’s, ENV’s and HOME’s to DRONE. Other pages leave them. While recording, pages leave the keys alone. What plays goes on playing.

The shape of a drone

ENV opens DRONE SHAPE: ATTACK (up to 30 s), RELEASE (up to 60 s), PRESSURE and VELOCITY.

LFO opens DRONE MOTION: MOTION and RATE (the drift), TIDE and PERIOD (the slow swell). Press LFO again for the three LFOs.

On every page: A KEYS, B PRESET, C BODY. TEMPO is on GLOBAL, K1.
