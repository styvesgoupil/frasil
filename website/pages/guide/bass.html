Playing BASS

Press SEQ, or turn SELECT (KEYS) to BASS. The keys play BASS and the badge turns orange. One note at a time, deep and steady. The bass stays out of the reverb.

The envelope and PULSE

BASS ADSR shapes each note: ATTACK (rise), DECAY (fall to SUSTAIN), SUSTAIN (level held), RELEASE (fade after you let go). At SUSTAIN 100 %, DECAY does nothing. Lower SUSTAIN to hear it.

The strip draws the curve. The part you turn lights up.

{{shot_bass_adsr}}

PULSE (BASS II, K4) retriggers the held note on the beat, from 1/8 BEAT to 4 BEATS. Short RELEASE and low SUSTAIN: a hard, gated pulse. Long RELEASE: a soft swell on each beat.

With PULSE on, a new note waits for the next pulse.

OCTAVE (BASS II, K2) moves everything the bass plays: keys, MIDI and sequence.

The sequencer

Turn SEQUENCE (BASS SEQ II, K2) ON, or tap REC with the keys on BASS. Up to sixteen steps play in turn, synced to MIDI clock or TEMPO. Each step is a NOTE, a CUT (silence) or a HOLD (the note goes on), with its own STEP LENGTH, from 1/16 to 16 BARS. SEQ STEPS (K1) sets how many steps play.

PLAY stops and restarts both sequencers, GLINT’s and the bass’s, together. Hold PLAY to stop everything; hold it again to bring it back.

RECORDWhat a BASS key does
OFFTransposes the sequence to that key, in the scale. TRANSPOSE (BASS SEQ II, K4) shows how far
STEPWrites its note on the current step, with the last STEP LENGTH, then moves on
LIVEPlays, and records the note as you played it, rounded to the nearest sixteenth

On BASS SEQ, K2 sets the current step’s NOTE (CUT fully left, HOLD fully right) and K3 its STEP LENGTH. CLEAR (turn twice) empties every step.

Tap REC with the keys on BASS to record LIVE: SEQUENCE turns on and BASS SEQ opens. Tap REC again to stop. On LIVE, a metronome ticks and REC flashes on the beat.

  • Over a sequence, the take loops round it. Each note replaces only the time it covers.
  • On an empty sequence, the take builds the loop a bar at a time. REC again ends it at the nearest bar line.
  • Sixteen steps at most. When full, the header says BASS SEQ FULL and the note joins its neighbour or is not recorded.

The strip draws the sequence as a line in time, each step as wide as it is long. The playing note is bright.

Each scene keeps its own bass sequence. While SEQUENCE is on, notes on BASS’s MIDI channel go into it like the keys.

On the small speaker

A low sine barely reaches the FM-1’s small speaker. Add DRIVE or pick a richer MODEL to hear it there. In headphones, SINE with DRIVE at 0 is a clean sub.
