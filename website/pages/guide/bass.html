Playing BASS

Turn SELECT, the KEYBOARD, to BASS: the keys play BASS (the badge turns orange). One note at a time, deep and steady: one of eight MODELs, DRIVE into a soft clip, through a resonant low-pass. None of it goes into the reverb.

GestureWhat happens
Tap a keyThe bass holds that note: the key’s note in the scale, OCTAVE octaves down. It rises over ATTACK
Tap another keyThe bass glides to its note (GLIDE), without starting again
Tap the bass’s keyIt lets go, over RELEASE
Hold its key and pressLouder, and its filter opens. Let go: it stays

With GLOBAL › KEYS on HOLD, the bass sounds while its key is down. Holding PLAY lets it go with everything else. It stays when you turn the keys back to drones or GLINT: SELECT on BASS again comes back to it.

The models

SINE
pure
TRIANGLE

SQUARE
hollow, woody
SAW
bright, buzzing
REESE
two saws ten cents apart, beating slowly
SUB
the sine and its octave below
ORGAN
three drawbars: the note, its octave and its twelfth
FM
a sine modulating itself: round, growling

Turning MODEL while the bass sounds crosses over in a few milliseconds, without a click.

Its pages

Tap SEQ, then SEQ again for the next. Hold SEQ for SWELL, as ever.

On the small speaker

A pure sine this low barely reaches the FM-1’s small speaker: DRIVE, or a richer MODEL, gives it the harmonics that let you hear it there. In headphones or a mixer, SINE with DRIVE at 0 is a clean sub.
