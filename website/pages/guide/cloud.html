GENERATIVE FX

FX opens on GENERATIVE FX: four knobs that play the whole sound rather than a part of it. SWELL is the hand’s; SPARKLE, BLUR and SINK belong to the sound, and a scene keeps them.

{{shot_genfx}}
KnobWhat it does
SWELL K1Every drone voice swelled, as far as it is turned, and held there. With SEQ held or CC 11 too, the most of them wins
SPARKLE K2Glints play by themselves, now and then, on the notes the drones hold: GLINT’s OCTAVE up, one in three an octave more, soft and short. From a few a minute to about six a second. Nothing held, nothing
BLUR K3The drone dissolves into the cloud: wetter (SPACE toward 90 %), a longer decay, more diffusion and modulation, the glints further into the cloud
SINK K4Everything goes under: a low-pass on the whole mix, from 12 kHz down to about 120 Hz, with a small resonance (2.4 kHz at 25 %, 800 Hz at 50 %)

An LFO can move SPARKLE, BLUR and SINK too (see The LFOs).

The cloud

WARMTH (a soft saturation) feeds CLOUD, the reverb: an 8-line modulated network with diffusion in its loop, a pre-delay, a pitch-shifted shimmer inside the loop, and FREEZE. Its tail runs from 0.4 to 60 seconds.

The drones flow into it; so does GLINT, by its own SPACE (GLINT DELAY, K4), and its delay’s echoes. BASS stays out of it, but for a little SPACE. The MIXER’s CLOUD fader is the reverb’s return.

FREEZE

Hold FX, on any page, for FREEZE, or turn CLOUD’s K4; the page stays. The cloud holds what is in it, without end and without darkening. Nothing more goes in: what you play next sounds dry over it. Off (FX held again): it thaws, and fades over DECAY. FX stays bright while it is on.

On the screen, FREEZE sets the crystals into a lattice and stills the water, and the header shows FREEZE while the cloud is held. On the logo screen, FREEZE stills it all.

Over MIDI, CC 80 freezes the cloud at 64 and up.

Room to breathe

If the sound crackles or a voice drops out, FRASIL is lowering its load on its own: fewer voices, or SHIMMER at 0, give it room. GLOBAL’s header shows the load.
