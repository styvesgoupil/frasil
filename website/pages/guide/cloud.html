GENERATIVE FX

FX opens on GENERATIVE FX: four knobs that act on the whole sound. A scene keeps SPARKLE, BLUR and SINK.

{{shot_genfx}}
KnobWhat it does
SWELL K1Swells every drone voice and holds it there
SPARKLE K2Glints play by themselves on the notes the drones hold
BLUR K3The drone dissolves into the cloud
SINK K4Everything goes under water: a low-pass on the whole mix

An LFO can move SPARKLE, BLUR and SINK (see The LFOs).

The cloud

CLOUD is the reverb, with shimmer and FREEZE. Its tail lasts up to 60 seconds.

The drones and GLINT go into it. The bass mostly stays out. The MIXER’s CLOUD fader sets its level.

Its pages

Press FX again for CLOUD, CLOUD II and CLOUD III. Then come MASTER FILTER, LOFI TAPE, PUMP and PUMP II (see Master filter, Lofi tape and Pump).

FREEZE

Hold FX on any page, or turn CLOUD’s K4. The cloud holds what is in it, forever. What you play next sounds dry over it. Hold FX again to let it fade.

The header shows FREEZE while it is on. MIDI CC 80 also freezes it.

Room to breathe

If the sound crackles or a voice drops out, FRASIL is overloaded. Use fewer voices, or set SHIMMER to 0. GLOBAL’s header shows the load.
