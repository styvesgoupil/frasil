The mixer

Press HOME on the play page: a fader and a live meter for each track, DRONE, BASS, GLINT and CLOUD (the reverb). K1–K4 move them. 100 is unity.

HOME again opens the logo screen.

Hold OCT− and turn K1–K4 to pan each track. A scene keeps the pans. An LFO can move them (see The LFOs).

Master filter

MASTER FILTER (FX, fifth page) is a low-pass or high-pass on the tracks you pick with TRACKS. An LFO can move CUTOFF and RESO (see The LFOs).

Lofi tape

LOFI TAPE (FX, sixth page) puts the tracks you pick on worn tape. SATURATION warms and dulls them without making them louder. WOBBLE adds wow, flutter and the odd drag.

Pump

PUMP (FX, seventh page) ducks the tracks on the beat, like a sidechain from a kick. RATE sets how often. DRONE, BASS and GLINT set how deep each one ducks.

PUMP II shapes it: RELEASE (how long it takes to come back), ATTACK (how fast it drops), OFFSET (where in the beat it drops; 50 % is the off-beat).

Routing

ROUTING (GLO, sixth page). FILTER and TAPE: POST CLOUD puts them after the reverb, PRE CLOUD before it, leaving the reverb clean. CLOUD PUMP: ON, PUMP ducks the reverb with the drone. A scene keeps these three.

SPK/JACK OFF mutes the speaker and headphone jack while USB audio goes on: record with the room quiet. The FM-1 keeps it through power-off.

Screen

SCREEN (GLO, seventh page): ANIMATIONS LOW stills the decorative animations. Meters and playheads still move.

Mastering

MASTERING (GLO, third page) is a compressor on the whole output. RATIO is OFF by default. GAIN is make-up gain. The page shows the gain reduction live.

Master EQ

MASTER EQ (GLO, fourth page) is a three-band EQ on the output. PRESET picks a starting curve; turn a band and it says CUSTOM. Turn PRESET between OFF and CUSTOM to compare with and without.

MASTER EQ II (GLO, fifth page) sets each band’s frequency, and the MID band’s width, Q.

FLAT
no change
WARM
more lows, softer highs
AIR
more highs
MUD CUT
less low-mid
BOX CUT
less boxiness
LOUD
lows and highs up
SMALL SPEAKER
lows cut, presence up

A scene keeps MASTER FILTER, LOFI TAPE, MASTER EQ and MASTERING.
