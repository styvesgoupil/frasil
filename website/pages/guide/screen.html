The river

  • The current is CHOIR. Louder, brighter.
  • The crystals are PARTIALS. Louder, more of them.
  • The mist is BREATH.
  • Pressure warms a voice to lamp light. GLINT notes shine in the sky. FREEZE stills the water.
  • The bass is an orange swell across the river at its note’s height, its note on the right.
  • SINK darkens the river from the top. BLUR thickens the haze.

The strata and the three hands

Under the waterline, four strata show the drone’s mix: CHOIR, PARTIALS, BREATH and WARMTH. K1–K4 set them.

At the top right, what is running: SEQ or REC, ARP, BASS SEQ or BASS REC, the three LFOs, SWELL. Lit while it runs, grey while it waits.

At the bottom, the three hands: KEYS (SELECT), the drone’s preset (PRESETS, 07/24 or CUSTOM) and BODY (ALGORITHM).

The badges

Each setting wears the badge of its control. Blue 1 2 3 4 are K1–K4. Orange A B C are SELECT, PRESETS and ALGORITHM.

A small ice-blue mark on a gauge shows where an LFO has it now. See The LFOs.

The header

On the play page: the scene’s name, an amber dot if the sound has changed since, and the key (D MINOR) if set. Elsewhere: the page’s name. The dots under it are the button’s pages.

At the right, the keys’ badge: what the keys play and the octave. DRONE white, GLINT ice blue, BASS orange. REC while the keys record. Four climbing dots while the arpeggiator has the keys. A solid frame: notes latch. Dotted: they sound while held.

Beside it: FREEZE while the cloud is held, and a brief amber dot when the engine lightened the sound (see crackles).

Messages show there for a moment: STORED 09 ABYSS, RELEASE ALL, a MORPH’s progress, COMPARE · AS SAVED.

A warning fills the screen (STOP FIRST, NOT STORED). Press SEL to close it. In every question, SEL is yes and FX is no; only those buttons are lit.

The mixer

Press HOME on the play page: four faders, DRONE, BASS, GLINT and CLOUD (the reverb), with live meters. K1–K4 move them. 100 is unity.

Hold OCT− and turn K1–K4: each track’s PAN. Scenes keep it.

The logo screen

HOME a third time: the logo, alive with what you play, and the version at the top right. You can play from it. HOME again: back to the play page.

The River at frasilsynth.com backs up and edits your scenes.
