Tap a key: a drone lights and stays. Press: it swells. The timbre drifts on its own; the notes never change. Everything flows into a large reverb. GLINT plays icy bells on top. BASS holds one deep note under.

The controls at a glance

ControlWhat it does
KeysTap: light a drone. Again: it fades. Press: it swells
SELECT · PRESETS · ALGORITHMOn every page: A KEYS (DRONE, GLINT, BASS), B the drone’s preset, C BODY
K1–K4The page’s four settings
RECRecord LIVE into GLINT’s sequencer (or the bass’s); again: off. Hold: metronome
ARPGLINT’s pages. Hold: arpeggio LATCH
SEQBASS’s pages
SELHARMONY. Hold + key: a single note
EDIT, ENV, LFO, FX, GLOThe drone and its layers, DRONE SHAPE, DRONE MOTION and the LFOs, the effects, GLOBAL. Hold: ENV SWELL, FX FREEZE, LFO + K1–K4 route to an LFO, GLO + CC MIDI learn. OCT− + EDIT: COMPARE
HOMEThe play page, MIXER, the logo. Hold: help
SAVESCENES; again: back. Hold + REC: STORE. Hold + white key: load that scene
PLAYStart or stop the sequences. With nothing playing: the scene. Twice or hold: RELEASE ALL. Hold with nothing playing: RESTORE. OCT− + PLAY: CUT
FREEZE (hold FX)The reverb holds what is in it. Also CLOUD’s K4
OCT− · OCT+Octave −3 to +2 (both: −1). Hold OCT− + turn a preset: pick it unheard. On MIXER, OCT− + K1–K4: pan

Buttons act when released; held, they do their other thing. See the buttons.
