SHAPE, DEPTH, POLARITY

SHAPE is the wave. DEPTH is how far it moves its targets; 0 is off. POLARITY: BIPOLAR moves around the setting, UP only above it, DOWN only below.

RATE: free, or on the beat

Turn RATE up from a 200-second turn to 10 Hz. Past that, it locks to the beat, from 16 BARS to 1/16.

On the beat, it follows MIDI clock, or TEMPO (GLOBAL, K1) without one.

The targets

Each LFO’s TARGETS page picks up to four settings to move, on K1–K4.

An LFO runs while DEPTH is above 0 and a TARGET is set. LFOs on the same setting add up.

From any page: LFO held and an encoder

Hold LFO and turn K1–K4 on any page. The setting under that knob goes onto LFO 1. Keep turning for LFO 2, LFO 3, then none. A DEPTH at 0 jumps to 25 %. The header confirms: FILTER > LFO 2, LFO 2 FULL or NOT AN LFO TARGET.

Around the setting

The LFO moves the sound around the value you set. A scene saves that value and the LFO settings.

Keys do not restart an LFO. It runs on its own under whatever you play.

GL BRIGHT and GL PING change at each new note. The other targets move continuously.

On the screen

A moving setting shows a small ice-blue mark on its gauge, where the LFO has it now.

On an LFO’s page, the strip draws its wave and where it is now. It turns grey while the LFO is off.

On a TARGETS page, names are coloured by track: GLINT ice blue, bass orange, drone and mix white.

A few to try

  • PEAK up on PARTIALS II, a slow SINE on FORMANT: the drone sings one overtone, then the next.
  • SINE on BS FILTER at 1/8: the bass wobbles on the beat.
  • RANDOM on GL BRIGHT, faster than the notes: each glint a different colour.
  • DRIFT on TIMBRE at 1:40: the drones glide to a new colour every 100 seconds.
