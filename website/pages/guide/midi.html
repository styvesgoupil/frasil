Channels

Press GLO twice for the MIDI page. DRONE CH, GLINT CH and BASS CH: each OFF, 1 to 16 or OMNI.

  • CH 1DRONE
  • CH 2GLINT
  • CH 3BASS

Notes

  • Drone notes go through the harmony: one note plays a chord.
  • GLINT notes play as sent, no scale. With the arpeggio or sequencer on, they feed it.
  • BASS notes play one at a time, the last one wins. With its sequence on, they feed it.

Notes sound only while held. Polyphonic aftertouch swells a note; channel pressure, the whole track.

MIDI learn

Turn a setting, hold GLO, and move a knob on your controller: that CC now turns that setting (CC 21 · SINK). Up to 8, the oldest replaced. A learned CC overrides the table, except 64, 120 and 123.

Program Change

Program Change 0 to 15, on the drones’ channel, loads scenes 1 to 16, with SCENES’ MORPH and LOAD.

Clock

MIDI clock drives the arpeggio, the sequencers, GLINT’s delay and the synced LFOs. Start restarts them from the top. CLOCK (MIDI page, K4): AUTO follows an incoming clock; INTERNAL ignores it and keeps TEMPO.

SysEx

Only a PERFORM page uses SysEx.
