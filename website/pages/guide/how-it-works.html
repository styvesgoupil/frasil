Three layers, one voice

  • CHOIR detuned saws through a filter. Warm, wide, vocal.
  • PARTIALS 8 drifting harmonics. Bright or dark, up to bells.
  • BREATH noise through resonators tuned to the note, from air to glass.

All three play every note. You mix and shape them on EDIT: two pages per layer. See the layers’ second pages.

The whole drone

EDIT’s first page, DRONE TONE: TIMBRE (brighter or darker), SPACE (how much reverb), MOTION (how much drift) and BODY. BODY at 100 % is the full drone; lower thins it out. ALGORITHM turns BODY on every page.

The drone’s presets

Turn PRESETS on any page: CUSTOM, then 24 drones. A preset sets the layers, DRONE TONE, ATTACK, RELEASE and DRONE MOTION. It leaves the cloud, PRESSURE, VELOCITY and BODY alone.

Turn any of those settings and the drone becomes CUSTOM. CUSTOM stays there while you try other presets. Scenes keep it.

What moves on its own

The drones’ notes never change. The sound does: MOTION drifts it, TIDE swells the timbre over minutes.

Chance comes in only where you let it: CHANCE, RANDOM and DRIFT on GLINT, SPARKLE on FX’s first page (see Generative FX).

Three LFOs move up to four settings each, free or on the beat. See The LFOs.

GLINT and BASS

GLINT plays on top: 4 voices of FM bells. Turn the keys to it with SELECT, or open ARP’s pages. Its notes ring and fade, never latch. Play it by hand, with its arpeggiator or its 32-step sequencer. See GLINT.

BASS plays under: one deep held note, or a line from its 16-step sequencer. Turn the keys to it with SELECT, or open SEQ’s pages. It stays nearly dry. See BASS.
