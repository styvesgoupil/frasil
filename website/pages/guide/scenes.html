Storing and loading

Press SAVE for SCENES. SAVE again goes back. K1 picks a line: NEW SHARD first, then the 16 slots. NOW marks the scene loaded.

Load: PLAY loads the slot K1 points at. If nothing is playing, it plays at once. From any page, hold SAVE and press a white key: the 16 white keys are the 16 scenes, from F2. While SAVE is held, the current scene’s key lights bright, the used slots faint. MIDI Program Change loads them too.

Store: hold SAVE and press REC. On SCENES, it goes into the slot K1 points at; elsewhere, into the current scene (STORED 09 ABYSS). The scene keeps the sound, TEMPO, the latched drones, GLINT’s arpeggio and sequence, and the bass’s note and sequence.

Name: after STORE comes NAME IT. OCT− and OCT+ move the cursor, K4 turns the letter, K3 the digit. SEL stores the name, FX skips. 16 characters at most.

The sound includes the LFOs, the effects, MASTER EQ, MASTERING, ROUTING and the MIXER’s pans. SWELL and KEYS are not kept.

MORPH and LOAD

MORPH (K2) is how a load comes in: CUT, at once, or a glide from 1 S to 2:00. Choices such as presets or SCALE switch at once. A knob you turn during a morph leaves it. A line in the header shows how far it has come.

LOAD (K3) is what a load brings. SND+NOTES: the sound, its TEMPO and its notes, the sequences from their first step. SND ONLY: the sound alone; what plays goes on, at the same TEMPO.

MORPH is back on CUT at power-on. LOAD is kept.

COMPARE

Hold OCT−, then hold EDIT: you hear the scene as saved (COMPARE · AS SAVED). Let go of EDIT to hear your changes.

NEW SHARD

The line above slot 01: a fresh start that plays nothing until you tap a key. A calm Am7 drone at 90 BPM, slow to come and go, in a lush cloud, the three LFOs moving it slowly. A new FM-1 starts on it.

Store it, changed or not, to make it your own: SAVE + REC on any other page puts it in the first EMPTY slot, then NAME IT. With K1 on NEW SHARD itself, nothing is stored.

Your scenes

Slots 8 to 16 are yours. An empty slot loads as NEW SHARD. When the sound differs from the scene, SAVE lights, SCENES says CHANGED and an amber dot shows by the scene’s name.

At power-on FRASIL comes back with the sound you stored last, silent. Tap PLAY to play it.

Scenes live in the FM-1’s flash, and another firmware may erase them: back them up before you switch (see where your scenes are kept). Scenes from an earlier FRASIL load as they sounded.

Backing up

FRASIL 1.1 and later back up the scenes and the settings to a file over USB.

  • In the browser: the River (Chrome or Edge). Back up shows each slot as a card, a shard. Download everything (.frasil) or the shards you select (.frasilscenes). Put a backup back whole, or shards into the slots you choose; the other slots stay. Shards need FRASIL 2.0 or later.
  • From a terminal: python3 base/tools/fm1_backup.py backup scenes.frasil --magic FRBK, then python3 base/tools/fm1_backup.py restore scenes.frasil; info shows what a file holds.

You can back up while playing; what you haven’t stored isn’t included. A restore needs silence: the River stops the sound itself; from a terminal, hold PLAY first (else STOP FIRST). The FM-1 then asks RESTORE FROM USB?: SEL yes, FX no. A full restore brings back the settings too, never the panel’s calibration.
