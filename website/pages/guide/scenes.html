Storing and loading

Press SAVE for SCENES; SAVE again goes back to the page you came from. K1 picks a slot, K2 to K4 are MORPH, KEEP and UNDO. The screen reminds you: PLAY LOAD, SAVE + REC STORE.

Load: on SCENES, PLAY loads the slot K1 points at. On any page, hold SAVE and press a white key: the 16 white keys are the 16 scenes, F2’s key the first. That key sounds nothing, and the page stays. While SAVE is held, the white keys light: the current scene’s bright, the slots that hold a scene faintly. MIDI Program Change loads them too (see MIDI). Every load comes in as MORPH and KEEP say.

Store: hold SAVE and press REC: the sound goes into the slot K1 points at on SCENES, into the current scene anywhere else (STORED 03 ABYSS), with the latched drones (up to 8 chords), GLINT’s arpeggio notes and its sequence, and the bass’s note. Into a free slot (9 to 16) with a drone preset on, the scene takes the preset’s name; else it is SCENE and its number.

UNDO (K4): turn it once (AGAIN), then once more: the last STORE undone, the slot back as it was.

The LFOs are part of the sound: a scene keeps their own settings, and each setting they move at the value you gave it. Each scene keeps its own GLINT sequence too, and the settings keep the last one saved.

A scene keeps the drone’s CUSTOM, and the preset chosen over it, and GENERATIVE FX’s SPARKLE, BLUR and SINK. SWELL and the KEYBOARD are the hand’s: scenes don’t keep them.

MORPH and KEEP

MORPH (K2) is how the sound comes in: CUT, at once, or over 1 S, 2 S, 4 S, 8 S, 15 S, 30 S, 60 S or 2:00. Over a time, the settings that glide go from where they are to the scene’s; the choices (the presets, MODEL, ROOT, SCALE, CHORD, RATIO, TYPE, the TARGETs, INTERVAL, the rates, TIME, LENGTH, TRANSPOSE, TUNE) switch at once. A setting you turn during the morph leaves it: your hand wins (a preset, all it sets). On the play page and SCENES, the header shows an arrow, the scene’s name and a line for how far it has come; on the other pages, only the line, under the page’s name. A new load starts from the values as they are.

KEEP (K3) is what happens to what plays. SCENE: everything is let go, then the scene’s notes play, if it has any. PLAYING: what plays goes on (the drones, the bass, GLINT’s arpeggio and sequence, with its TYPE and the settings that play it: MODE, RATE, RANGE, CHANCE, LENGTH, GATE, TRANSPOSE); only the sound changes.

MORPH and KEEP are kept with the settings, not in the scenes.

COMPARE

Hold EDIT. While it is held, the scene sounds as it was saved or last loaded, whatever you have turned since; the header says COMPARE · AS SAVED. Let go: the sound as it is now.

Your scenes

Slots 9 to 16 are free. While the sound differs from the one last loaded or saved, SAVE lights, the SCENES page says CHANGED and the play page’s header has an amber dot beside the scene’s name (back to it, they go). Storing also keeps the settings: at power-on FRASIL comes back with the sound you stored last, silent. Nothing plays until you play.

Scenes saved by an earlier FRASIL come back as they were, the settings added since at their defaults; their drone comes in as CUSTOM, with its sound, and GLINT’s arpeggio, sequence and delay move to their new pages as they sounded.
