DRONE TONE

TIMBRE makes the drone brighter or darker. SPACE sends more of it to the reverb. MOTION sets how far it drifts. BODY at 100 % leaves the drone as set; turn it down to thin it out. ALGORITHM turns BODY from any page. A preset sets this page, except BODY (see the drone’s presets).

CHOIR II

SHAPE turns each saw into a hollow square at the middle, then a bright, nasal pulse. INTERVAL moves the centre saw an octave down, a fifth up or an octave up.

RESONANCE adds a peak to the filter, up to a whistle. TYPE picks the filter: LOW (warm), BAND (nasal, vocal) or HIGH (thin, airy).

The wave you hear is drawn above the page: SAW, SQUARE in the middle, then a narrower PULSE.

CHOIR III

SOFT rounds the choir's wave off: a triangle at 50 %, near a sine at 100 %, at the same level. FILTER still darkens it on top.

PARTIALS II

EVOLVE makes the partials come and go on their own. BEAT makes each one pulse, slow to quick: a shimmer.

FORMANT and PEAK lift the partials around one harmonic, like a vowel. FORMANT picks the harmonic, H1 to H8. PEAK sets how strongly; at the top, the drone sings that one overtone. Sweep FORMANT with PEAK up, by hand or with an LFO.

BREATH II

GRAIN breaks the hiss into sparse grains, like ice ticking or rain on glass. PITCH moves the resonators up or down: a halo over the drone. TONE makes the noise darker or brighter. AIR adds raw noise beside the resonators.

The processor

SHAPE, SOFT, RESONANCE, TYPE, BEAT and AIR cost more processing. With many voices and several of these up, FRASIL thins out partials, then voices, to keep the sound going.
