Playing GLINT

Press ARP, or turn SELECT (KEYS) to GLINT. The keys play GLINT and the badge turns blue. Each key plays an icy bell in the scale, OCTAVE octaves up. Press harder: louder and longer.

VIBRATO (GLINT II, K4): press in, then wiggle your finger to bend the pitch. A steady press stays in tune.

Its pages

Tap ARP again for the next page: GLINT I, GLINT II, GLINT ADSR, GLINT DELAY. SEQ (GLINT I, K2) picks what plays GLINT besides your hands: OFF, ARP or SEQ. Their pages follow GLINT DELAY.

The arpeggiator and the sequencer share RATE and CHANCE.

OCTAVE moves everything GLINT plays: keys, MIDI, arpeggio and sequence.

Presets

ICE, BELL, BLIP, GLASS, CHIME, DROP, FROST, CELESTA, SINE, KALIMBA, MARIMBA, MUSIC BOX, VIBES, HARP, PIPE, BOWED, PIANO, and six leads: WARM LEAD, BRASS, SQUARE, SUB LEAD, STRINGS, REED. Pick one, then adjust from there.

Hold OCT− and turn PRESET to browse without hearing it (the value turns amber). Let OCT− go to load it.

The envelope

GLINT ADSR shapes each note: ATTACK (rise), DECAY (fall), SUSTAIN (level held while the key is down), RELEASE (ring after you let go).

The strip draws the curve. The part you turn lights up. Each sounding note rides it as a small glint.

{{shot_glint_adsr}}

SUSTAIN: a bell becomes a lead

At SUSTAIN 0, a glint fades over DECAY, even while held: a bell. Turn SUSTAIN up and it holds as long as the key is down: a lead. The lead presets work this way.

The arpeggiator

With SEQ on ARP, held GLINT keys play one at a time, in MODE’s order, at RATE, over RANGE octaves. CHANCE skips some steps. It follows MIDI clock, or TEMPO (GLOBAL, K1). A new chord starts on the next step.

Latch: hold ARP. The arpeggio keeps playing after you lift your hands. A new chord replaces it. Hold ARP again to turn latch off. Tap PLAY to pause the arpeggio, again to restart it. Hold PLAY to stop everything.

The sequencer

With SEQ on SEQ, a sequence of up to 32 steps plays GLINT, at RATE, with CHANCE. PLAY stops it; PLAY again restarts it from the top.

Tap REC to record LIVE: the sequencer turns on, the keys play GLINT, and GLINT SEQ opens. Tap REC again to stop recording. While RECORD is on, the keys play GLINT.

On LIVE, a metronome ticks, higher on the first step, and REC flashes on the beat. Hold REC to turn the metronome off or on.

On GLINT SEQ, K2 sets the current step’s NOTE (REST fully left, TIE fully right) and K3 its ACCENT. A TIE holds the previous note on. GATE sets how long each note sounds.

CLEAR, NEW TAKE and UNDO TAKE: turn twice. CLEAR empties every step. NEW TAKE empties it and starts recording LIVE. UNDO TAKE brings back the sequence from before the last take or CLEAR; again to redo.

The strip draws the sequence as stars, one per step, as high as its note. The playing step is white. While recording, the step being written is lit in ice.

Each scene keeps its own sequence.

The delay

GLINT DELAY is GLINT’s own ping-pong delay. TIME follows MIDI clock or TEMPO. Its echoes go into the cloud too. SPACE (K4) sets how much of GLINT goes to the reverb.
