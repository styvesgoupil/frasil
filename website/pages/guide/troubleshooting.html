No sound

Turn MASTER up and tap a key: drones need a tap. Check the levels on HOME. If the keys’ badge says BASS or GLINT, turn SELECT back to DRONE.

Everything went silent

PLAY held, or tapped twice, lets everything go; OCT− held and PLAY (CUT) cuts it at once. Hold or tap PLAY again before you play anything: RESTORE brings it back.

KEY MODE doesn’t change

Turn it twice the same way within 2 seconds (KEY MODE · TURN AGAIN).

A CC moves the wrong setting

It was learned. Learn it again onto the setting you want.

Crackles, or a voice dropping out

Under heavy load, FRASIL drops voices to stay clean; an amber dot by the keys’ badge says so. Use fewer voices, or SHIMMER at 0.

Screen lagging: set ANIMATIONS to LOW (GLO, SCREEN).

USB recordings crackled before FRASIL 2.5: update.

The installer doesn’t find the FM-1

Use Chrome or Edge, allow MIDI, and close other programs using the FM-1.

PERFORM doesn’t find the FM-1

As for the installer; for COMPUTER AUDIO, allow the audio input too. NO ANSWER: update to FRASIL 2.5 or later. THIS COMPUTER PLAYS THROUGH THE FM-1: set the computer’s output to its own speakers, or choose FM-1 AUDIO.

The FM-1 doesn’t start after an install

Hold OCT− while you switch it on, then install again, or put the official firmware back.

The emulator is silent

Click Switch on. The page must be served over https or from your own computer.
